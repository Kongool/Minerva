Clipper2
--------

Minerva vendors the Clipper2 polygon-clipping library, unmodified, under
Minerva.Core/ThirdParty/Clipper2/. It is used to compose arena shapes and AOEs
from boolean operations (union, intersection, difference).

    Author    : Angus Johnson
    Copyright : Angus Johnson 2010-2025
    Website   : https://www.angusj.com
    License   : Boost Software License 1.0
                https://www.boost.org/LICENSE_1_0.txt

The source is kept byte-identical to upstream so it can be updated by replacing
the files; the global usings it expects are supplied by Minerva.Core.csproj
rather than by editing the source.

The wrappers around it under Minerva.Core/Geometry/Polygon/ (PolygonClipper,
RelSimplifiedComplexPolygon, RelPolygonWithHoles, EarCut, PolygonBoundaryIndex2D,
SpatialIndex, Triangle, PolygonUtil) are ported from BossmodReborn (BSD-3) and
are covered by the BossmodReborn notice above.
